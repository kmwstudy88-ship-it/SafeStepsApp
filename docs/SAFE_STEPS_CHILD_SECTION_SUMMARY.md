# SAFE STEPS — COMPLETE CHILD SECTION SUMMARY

The Child Section is a fully isolated, child-controlled environment designed around safety, choice, expression, and guided emotional development.

Parents cannot see anything inside this section unless the child explicitly chooses to share.

## 1. Child Dashboard

The dashboard shows the child everything relevant for today:

- Today’s Feelings Check-In
- Today’s Lessons
- Today’s Tasks
- My Story
- Visit Reflection
- My Safe People
- My Achievements
- Requests: Game, Talk, Help
- Shared Items Log

## 2. Child Curriculum

The child curriculum is completely separate from the parent curriculum.

It includes:

- Emotional Regulation
- Understanding Feelings
- Communication Skills
- Healthy Boundaries
- Safety Skills
- Visit Preparation
- Visit Reflection
- Self-Esteem Building
- Trauma-Informed Modules
- Age-Specific Learning Tracks

Each program contains:

- Weeks
- Lessons
- Tasks
- Evidence
- Optional Sharing

## 3. Child Tasks

Tasks are interactive, child-friendly, and trauma-informed.

Examples:

- Feelings check-ins
- Drawing tasks
- Voice note tasks
- Safe non-identifying photo tasks
- Reflection tasks
- Visit feedback tasks
- What I want my parent to work on
- What made me happy today
- What made me worried today

Tasks can be completed privately or shared.

## 4. Child Evidence

Children can upload or create:

- Drawings
- Voice notes
- Photos
- Written reflections
- Achievement badges
- Visit feedback

Evidence is:

- Time-stamped
- Linked to tasks
- Linked to lessons
- Share-controlled
- Role-restricted

Parents never see evidence unless the child shares it.

## 5. Child Sharing Controls

Children can choose to share:

- A feeling
- A completed lesson
- A completed task
- A message
- A game request
- A talk request
- A visit reflection
- A parent improvement request

Sharing options:

- Share with parent
- Share with caseworker
- Share with both
- Share with no one

Child control is absolute.

## 6. Child Notifications

Children can send:

- Game Request
- Talk Request
- Help Request
- I shared something with you
- I want you to work on something

These appear in the parent’s Parent-Child Section and on their dashboard.

## 7. Child Lessons

Each lesson contains:

- Title
- Description
- Learning objectives
- Tasks
- Evidence requirements
- Optional sharing
- Completion logic

Lessons are age-appropriate and trauma-informed.

## 8. Child Visit Tools

### Visit Preparation

Helps the child prepare emotionally for a visit.

### Visit Reflection

After a visit, the child can reflect on:

- What went well
- What felt uncomfortable
- What made them happy
- What made them worried
- What they want their parent to work on

The child can share or keep private.

## 9. My Story

A safe personal space containing:

- My feelings
- My drawings
- My achievements
- My safe people
- My hopes
- My worries
- My visit reflections
- My shared items

This is never visible to parents unless shared.

## 10. Child Safety Rules

Parents cannot see:

- Child curriculum
- Child tasks
- Child feelings
- Child evidence
- Child assessments
- Child visit reflections
- Child progress

Parents only see what the child chooses to share.

Caseworkers have full visibility according to their authorised role.
