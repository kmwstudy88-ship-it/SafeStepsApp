# Emotion adaptation and crisis safety

Emotion analysis is a non-diagnostic communication aid. It may adapt pacing and wording, but must never be described as a clinical measurement, certified counselling, proof of deterioration, or evidence about parenting capacity. Scores are ephemeral unless the user separately opts into a 90-day trend snapshot. Trend rows contain scores, version and timestamps—not message text—and are user-owned under RLS and deletable with consent revocation.

Explicit and reviewed indirect self-harm phrases use locked Australian crisis templates before any provider call. Embedding similarity alone is not authorized to make a crisis determination: thresholds can produce false positives across cultures, idioms, quotations and negations. Any future semantic classifier requires a versioned validation dataset, calibrated thresholds, subgroup testing, clinical review, fallback behaviour and monitoring before it can influence routing.

SafeSteps does not claim HIPAA or GDPR compliance from a database extension. Hosted Supabase encryption, RLS and minimal storage are technical controls; healthcare deployments additionally require appropriate legal agreements, high-compliance configuration, MFA, SSL enforcement, network restrictions, connection logging, incident processes and independent review.
