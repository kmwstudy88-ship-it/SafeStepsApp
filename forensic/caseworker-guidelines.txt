SAFE-STEPS CASEWORKER GUIDELINES

1. Tier 1 Events
   - Review within 48 hours
   - No direct intervention required
   - Document patterns if repeated

2. Tier 2 Events
   - Review within 24 hours
   - Consider contacting parent
   - Monitor escalation indicators

3. Tier 3 Events
   - Immediate review required
   - Contact child safety team
   - Document event in case plan
   - Consider temporary channel block

4. System-Blocked Channels
   - Review reason for block
   - Assess child safety risk
   - Decide whether to maintain block

5. Child-Paused Channels
   - Respect child's autonomy
   - Do not notify parent of pause reason
   - Review for emotional safety concerns

6. Forensic Reports
   - Use for court, case plans, supervision
   - Never alter message content
   - Ensure escalation indicators are accurate
