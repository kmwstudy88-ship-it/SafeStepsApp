# Financial Stability Evidence Framework

This framework helps SafeSteps track whether a parent can sustain the basic financial conditions needed for a safe and stable home. It should be used as evidence of patterns over time, not as a poverty penalty. The focus is prioritisation, planning, follow-through, and child needs being met.

## What To Collect and When

| Evidence type | Frequency | What it helps show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rent, mortgage, or housing statement | Monthly | Housing stability and payment priority |
| Power or electricity bill plus payment evidence | Monthly | Utilities maintained for child safety |
| Gas bill plus payment evidence | Monthly | Heating and cooking capacity |
| Water bill plus payment evidence | Monthly | Hygiene and sanitation capacity |
| Food shopping receipts | Weekly | Regular and adequate food provision |
| Child-specific purchases | As they occur | Child needs being prioritised |
| Internet or phone bill | Monthly | Communication and service connectivity |
| Centrelink, income, or payslip statement | Monthly | Income stability and financial management capacity |
| Budget plan | Weekly or fortnightly | Planning, prioritisation, and ability to anticipate expenses |
| Debt-service contact evidence | As needed | Engagement with debt management supports |
| Financial counselling attendance | If referred | Follow-through with professional support |
| Savings evidence | Monthly if applicable | Forward planning, even where amounts are small |

## Food Shopping Receipt Analysis

Food receipts should be reviewed for pattern and adequacy, not judged against unrealistic standards.

| Indicator | What it may show |
| --- | --- |
| Fresh fruit and vegetables present | Nutritional awareness and planning |
| Age-appropriate child food | Child-focused shopping |
| Adequate quantities | Planning, budgeting, and household provision |
| Regular consistent shopping | Routine and stability |
| Balanced essential items | Ability to prioritise food, hygiene, and household basics |
| High alcohol or non-essential ratio | Possible priority, lifestyle, or risk concerns requiring discussion |

## Document Submission Requirements

Minimum submission should include:

- Photo of bill showing amount due
- Photo of receipt, bank statement, or payment confirmation showing payment made
- Timestamped upload
- Camera-only upload where available
- Same-day receipt photo for cash purchases
- Notes field explaining partial payments, payment plans, hardship arrangements, or support used

Avoid requiring parents to expose unnecessary private information. Where possible, allow redaction of unrelated transactions while preserving date, payee, amount, and payment status.

## Financial Stability Scoring Rubric

| Score | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Bills unpaid, no reliable food shopping evidence, financial situation unmanaged or chaotic |
| 2 | Inconsistent evidence; some bills paid, irregular shopping, limited planning or follow-through |
| 3 | Most priority bills paid or managed, regular food shopping demonstrated, budget mostly realistic |
| 4 | Bills consistently paid or on active plans, regular nutritious shopping, child items prioritised, evidence shows forward planning |

## Additional Financial Indicators

Track these alongside the core score:

- Budget plan submitted and updated
- Debt management support engaged where needed
- Centrelink or income obligations maintained
- Financial counselling attendance where referred
- Savings, emergency fund, or planned payment buffer
- Child-specific purchases such as clothing, school supplies, bedding, hygiene items, medications, transport, or activity needs
- Evidence of using hardship programs before crisis

## Anti-Gaming Measures

| Risk | Counter-measure |
| --- | --- |
| Submitting old receipts | Require timestamped camera upload and check receipt date |
| Borrowing money just before submission | Cross-reference bank/payment patterns over time |
| Shopping once for show | Require weekly pattern evidence |
| Paying one bill only when monitored | Monthly longitudinal tracking across multiple bills |
| Hiding arrears or disconnections | Ask for statements showing due date, status, and payment plan if applicable |
| Overstating income stability | Compare income statements, Centrelink records, payslips, and budget plan |

## Evidence Report Use

This framework can build a 12-18 week timeline showing:

- Whether financial management improves, remains stable, or deteriorates
- Whether the parent can sustain housing and utilities
- Whether food and child-specific needs are consistently prioritised
- Whether the parent uses supports early rather than waiting for crisis
- Whether child protection concerns around neglect and basic needs have reduced or remain active

## Report Summary Fields

- Reporting period
- Number of expected submissions
- Number of completed submissions
- Missed or late submissions
- Bill status summary
- Food provision pattern
- Child-specific purchase evidence
- Budget plan status
- Support-service engagement
- Risk indicators
- Protective indicators
- Overall financial stability score
