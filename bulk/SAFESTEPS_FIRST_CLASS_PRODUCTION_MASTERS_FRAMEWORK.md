# SafeSteps First-Class Production Masters Framework
## Reunification & Parent Program Operational, Clinical & Digital Blueprint

---

## A. Master Program Architecture

### A1. Core System Pillars

1. **Safety First:** Protective capacity and risk reduction override all other domains.
2. **Evidence Integrity:** Every score must be supported by observable, documented evidence.
3. **Longitudinal Change:** No single assessment determines readiness; change must be sustained.
4. **Anti‑Gaming Safeguards:** Multi‑method, multi‑observer, multi‑context verification.
5. **Child-Centered Practice:** Child needs, voice, and safety anchor all decisions.
6. **Court-Ready Documentation:** Every component must withstand legal scrutiny.
7. **Digital Automation:** Scoring, evidence capture, and reporting must be automated and auditable.

---

## B. Clinical Governance Master Layer

### B1. Governance Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Authority |
|------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Clinical Director** | Final authority on safety, risk, reunification recommendations, policy exceptions | Final sign-off on all exit reports; override authority for safety |
| **Program Manager** | Operational oversight, facilitator supervision, quality control, collateral coordination | Approve facilitator assignments; escalate concerns; manage resources |
| **Facilitators** | Deliver program, score rubrics, record evidence, conduct home visits, manage collateral contacts | Administer assessments; complete weekly scoring; flag concerns |
| **Psychologists/Specialists** | Administer Tier 3 & Tier 4 assessments; interpret external tools; clinical consultation | Licensed professional opinions; external tool administration |
| **Quality Assurance Lead** | Monthly audits, scoring calibration, evidence integrity checks, outcome tracking | Audit sampling; recommend retraining; flag systemic issues |

### B2. Governance Rules (Non-Negotiable)

- [ ] All Tier 3 and Tier 4 assessments require licensed professionals only
- [ ] No facilitator may override risk indicators with engagement scores
- [ ] All contradictions must be logged and scored on consistency anchor
- [ ] All DV indicators trigger mandatory supervisor review within 24 hours
- [ ] All substance-use escalations trigger immediate pause and clinical reassessment
- [ ] All child distress during contact triggers pause and child safety review
- [ ] All unsafe home visit conditions trigger case manager notification
- [ ] No exit report may be completed without Clinical Director review
- [ ] No score may be changed without version history logged

## D. Anti-Gaming Master Safeguards

### D1. Multi-Source Verification Protocol

Every domain must be verified across minimum 3 sources:

| Source | Collection Method | Frequency |
|--------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Parent self-report** | Weekly reflections, interviews, questionnaires | Every session |
| **Worker observation** | Facilitator notes, session participation, behavioral rubric | Every session |
| **Collateral contacts** | GP, housing, school, support worker reports | Fortnightly |
| **Home visit evidence** | Unannounced or planned home observation | Weeks 5, 10, 16 minimum |
| **Video-coded interaction** | DPICS, CARE-Index, Emotional Availability (if contact authorized) | Weeks 7–8, 14 minimum |
| **External professional reports** | Psychologist assessments, substance-use screening, medical records | Intake, midpoint, exit |

### D2. Anti-Gaming Indicators (High-Risk Patterns)

| Indicator | Red Flag | Action |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Rehearsed narratives** | Same wording across multiple sessions; lacks spontaneity | Flag in consistency anchor; document examples |
| **Inconsistency across sessions** | Different story to facilitator vs collateral; changing explanations | Log each version; score consistency 1–2 |
| **Behavioral-verbal mismatch** | Says one thing; does another; words vs actions don't align | Document examples; score consistency 1–2 |
| **Stress-response collapse** | Regulation strategy vanishes under pressure; aggressive/shutdown reaction | Flag as risk; do not rate regulation >2 |
| **Over-compliance masking risk** | Attendance perfect but understanding shallow; homework done but not applied | Document shallow engagement; do not score engagement 4 |
| **Partner coaching or interference** | Parent speaks differently alone vs with partner; inconsistent story with partner present | Log partner interference; flag for DV assessment |
| **Selective memory** | Minimises risk history; denies prior statements; "forgets" removal reasons | Log contradictions; score insight/accountability low |

### D3. Automated Anti-Gaming Tools

**Contradiction Detection Prompts:**
- System flags when parent gives contradictory information (e.g., "no substance use" vs collateral "daily use")
- Facilitator must resolve or log as contradiction

**Stress-Response Markers:**
- Alert when parent shows escalation, shutdown, or defensive response in session
- Prompt: Document behavioral response and score consistency

**Behavioral-Verbal Alignment Scoring:**
- Automatic prompt: "Does stated parenting philosophy align with observed behavior?"
- Facilitator must provide evidence for answer

**Generalisation Evidence Requirement:**
- Cannot score skill ≥3 without real-world evidence
- System prompts: "Evidence of application outside program setting?"

**Collateral Mismatch Flags:**
- System compares parent self-report to collateral contact data
- Alert: "Self-report contradicts collateral information"

## E. Assessment Tier Master System

### E1. Tier 1 — Program Rubrics (SafeSteps Native)

**Tools:** 7-domain engagement rubric, engagement tracking, behavioral checklist, reflection journal scoring

**Who:** Facilitators

**Frequency:** Weekly (minimum)

**Scoring:** 1–4 anchored scale per domain

**Evidence:** Behavioral observation, homework, reflection journals, attendance

**Quality Check:** Supervisor review of 10% monthly

---

### E2. Tier 2 — Structured Observations (Trained Facilitators)

**Tools:** DPICS (parent-child communication), CARE-Index (sensitivity, structuring, non-intrusiveness), Emotional Availability Scales, Home Visit Observation Checklist, NCFAS-G family functioning tool

**Who:** Trained facilitators or external raters

**Frequency:** Weeks 7–8 (video), Weeks 5, 10, 16 (home visits)

**Scoring:** Tool-specific anchors; facilitator-scored or external rater

**Evidence:** Video transcripts, observation notes, behavioral examples

**Quality Check:** Supervisor spot-check; inter-rater reliability check on 20% of cases

---

### E3. Tier 3 — Psychological Assessments (Licensed Professionals Only)

**Tools:**
- PAI or MMPI-3 (personality screening)
- WAIS-IV (cognitive functioning)
- AAPI-2 (parenting attitudes)
- PSI-4 (parenting stress)
- TSI-2 (trauma impact)
- AAI (attachment style)
- SDM (risk assessment)
- AUDIT/DAST-10 + urinalysis (substance use)
- Formal Parenting Capacity Assessment (PCA)

**Who:** Registered psychologist or appropriately credentialed clinician

**Frequency:** Intake, midpoint (if indicated), exit

**Scoring:** Per tool manual; licensed administrator only

**Evidence:** Score summaries, interpretive reports, uploaded documentation

**Quality Check:** Clinical Director review of all Tier 3 reports; verification against original administration

---

### E4. Tier 4 — Forensic Assessments (Court-Ordered)

**Tools:** Forensic Parenting Capacity Assessment, expert testimony protocols

**Who:** Forensic psychologist or court-appointed expert

**Frequency:** Exit only if case contested or high complexity

**Scoring:** Forensic standards; expert opinion

**Evidence:** Comprehensive report; court-ready language; expert qualifications documented

**Quality Check:** Clinical Director review before submission to court

## F. Reunification Decision Master Matrix

### F1. Critical Domains (Must Be ≥3 for "Ready" Recommendation)

1. **Protective Capacity** — Child's safety prioritized independently
2. **Risk Factor Reduction** — Active risks reduced below thresholds
3. **Emotional Regulation** — Parent regulates stress without harm risk
4. **Insight & Accountability** — Understanding of removal reasons; responsibility taken
5. **Consistency Anchor** — Integrity of change evidence; alignment across sources
6. **Generalisation** — Skills demonstrated outside program setting
7. **Support Networks** — Stable, reliable, safe supports in place

### F2. Automatic "Not Ready" Conditions

**Any of the following = "Not Ready" recommendation (no exceptions):**

- [ ] Any critical domain = 1 (even if others are 4)
- [ ] Two or more critical domains = 2
- [ ] DV escalation during program
- [ ] Substance-use escalation (baseline → exit scores increasing)
- [ ] Child distress or fear during contact observations
- [ ] Unsafe household conditions during home visit
- [ ] Active partner interference or coercion
- [ ] Refusal of required services
- [ ] New crisis or child protection concern mid-program
- [ ] Consistency anchor = 1

### F3. "Ready" Recommendation Criteria

**All of the following must be present:**

- [ ] ≥5 critical domains ≥3
- [ ] Protective capacity ≥3 (non-negotiable)
- [ ] Risk Factor Reduction ≥3 (non-negotiable)
- [ ] Consistency anchor ≥3 (non-negotiable)
- [ ] Generalisation evidence documented in all domains
- [ ] Home visit and contact observations positive
- [ ] Collateral reports aligned with program evidence
- [ ] Child voice/readiness documented
- [ ] Support network confirmed and engaged
- [ ] Conditions for ongoing safety documented

## G. Workforce Competency Master Framework

### G1. Required Core Competencies

**All facilitators must demonstrate:**

| Competency | Standard | Verification |
|------------|----------|--------------|
| **Trauma-informed practice** | Understand developmental trauma impact; respond with safety, trustworthiness, choice, collaboration | Training certification + annual refresher |

| **Anti-gaming detection** | Identify inconsistencies, coached narratives, behavioral-verbal mismatch | Training + case scenarios; supervisor feedback |
| **Court-ready documentation** | Evidence-based writing; clear language; defensible reasoning | Training + document review; supervisor feedback |

### G2. Annual Competency Requirements

- [ ] Competency reassessment (supervisor-led case review)

- [ ] Rubric calibration (group scoring exercise; inter-rater reliability check)

## H. Quality Assurance Master System

### H1. Monthly QA Protocol

| Check | Method | Action on Finding |
|-------|--------|-------------------|
| **10% case audit** | Random sampling; full score-evidence review | Flag misalignment; retrain if needed |
| **Score-evidence alignment** | Does evidence support score? Is anchor met? | Return to facilitator if misaligned |
| **Collateral verification** | Contact collateral; confirm contact notes recorded accurately | Flag if collateral differs significantly |
| **Consistency anchor review** | Is consistency scored correctly? Are contradictions logged? | Retrain on consistency scoring |
| **Scoring calibration** | Group review of difficult cases; discuss anchor application | Document consensus; adjust if needed |

### H2. Annual QA Review

- [ ] Full rubric recalibration (all facilitators score same scenario; compare)
- [ ] Review of reunification outcomes (follow-up: how many stayed reunified?)
- [ ] Policy updates (new research, legal changes, tool updates)
- [ ] Digital system audit (data integrity, security, backup verification)
- [ ] Workforce competency reassessment (all facilitators re-certified)

### H3. QA Decision Rules

| Finding | Action |
|---------|--------|
| **Score-evidence misalignment ≥30%** | Immediate retraining; 100% supervision for 2 months |
| **Consistency scoring errors ≥20%** | Retraining; recalibration with supervisor |
| **Collateral mismatch ≥25%** | Review collateral protocols; audit communication logs |
| **Digital audit failure** | Halt new case enrollment; IT investigation; data recovery check |

## I. Court-Ready Evidence Master Standards

### I1. Required Exit Report Elements

**All 15 elements mandatory; none can be omitted:**

1. **Title and administrative details:** Program name, dates, parent/child names (or ID), assessor name/credential, legal case reference
2. **Executive summary:** Clear reunification readiness statement; key safety findings; conditions if any
3. **Parent and child context:** Family structure, removal history, court order dates, legal status, prior service history
4. **Assessment timeline:** All tools (Tier 1–4), dates administered, order of administration, assessor credentials, administration setting
5. **Baseline profile (Week 1):** Intake scores all 7 domains; risk indicators identified; protective strengths noted
6. **Program participation:** Attendance %, homework completion %, engagement quality, any absences/non-compliance documented
7. **Phase-by-phase trajectory:** Domain scores at Week 1, 4, 10, 16, 18 in tabular format; visual graphs
8. **Domain-by-domain narrative:** For each of 7 domains:
   - Baseline status
   - Trajectory of change (which phases showed most change)
   - Key evidence with specific behavioral examples
   - Current status at exit
   - Assessment of sustainability
9. **Consistency analysis:** Consistency anchor score; internal consistency evidence; behavioral-verbal alignment examples; generalisation evidence documented
10. **Parent-child interaction:** Contact observation summaries; video-coded results with specifics (e.g., "DPICS: parent used 4 labeled praises, 0 commands during 20-minute play session"); repair evidence; relationship trajectory
11. **Risk indicators:** What risk factors present at intake? Which reduced? Which remain? Which new? Evidence for each (baseline vs exit scores, collateral reports, screening results)
12. **Protective capacities:** Examples of demonstrated protective action; network resilience; examples of child-centered decision-making
13. **Limitations of report:** What contexts were not observed? Which tools were not used and why? Assessor qualifications stated; assessment scope defined
14. **Professional recommendations:** Clear reunification readiness statement ("Recommended for reunification with the following conditions:" or "Not recommended for reunification because:"); ongoing support needs; follow-up plan; any conditions on contact frequency
15. **Appendices:** Score tables, observation checklists, video-coding summaries, external reports (PAI, WAIS-IV, psychologist assessments), contradiction logs, collateral contact summaries

### I2. Court Language Rules (Non-Negotiable)

- [ ] **No speculation** — "Parent appears to..." = poor. "Parent demonstrated in 6 of 8 home visits..." = good.
- [ ] **No assumptions** — "Parent will likely..." = poor. "Parent has maintained for 16 weeks..." = good.
- [ ] **Evidence only** — Every statement supported by specific examples, dates, or tool results
- [ ] **Clear, defensible** — Written for judge/jury; no jargon without definition
- [ ] **Distinguish sources** — "Parent stated..." vs "Collateral reported..." vs "Facilitator observed..."
- [ ] **Acknowledge limits** — "This assessment does not include overnight contact observation; further assessment recommended before unrestricted overnight visits."
