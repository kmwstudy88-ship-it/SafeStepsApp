Weekly Session Flow (Suggested 5-Week Schedule)
Week 1 (Mod 1): Focus on front-loading rules and differentiating logical consequences from punishments.

Week 2 (Mod 2): Practice the Regulate → Relate → Reason sequence via roleplay.

Week 3 (Mod 3): Guide parents through creating a sample Restorative Agreement Contract.

Week 4 (Mod 4): Audit current parental control levels using the I Do, We Do, You Do release framework.

Week 5 (Capstone): Review draft entries for The Complete Family Accountability Manual and grade scenario assessments.
